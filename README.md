# GlanceDesk

**Intelligence on Your Desk** 

GlanceDesk is a compact, battery-powered desktop cube with a zero-glare e-paper display, ambient light indicator, and micro-speakers. It delivers real-time info—public transport timings, developer AI task progress, weather, and sleep audio—at a glance without forcing you to switch browser tabs or open your phone.

# Key features
1. **At-a-Glance Dashboard:** Live LTA bus arrival countdowns, local weather updates, to-do lists, and calendar events. Zero taps required.
2.  **Developer AI Agent Mode:** Real-time visibility into AI agent execution progress, terminal tasks, and API/token costs directly on your desk.
3.  **Night Mode:** Functions as an auto-off night light with ambient sleep audio / pink noise generation.
4. **Portable & Low Friction:** Battery-powered, ultra-low power e-paper display designed to go anywhere in the house or while traveling.
