# Product Overview: GlanceDesk

> **Intelligence on Your Desk**

Trackuboid is a compact, battery-powered desktop cube equipped with a zero-glare e-paper display, ambient light indicator, and micro-speakers. It delivers ambient, at-a-glance information throughout your day—eliminating phone screen time and browser tab switching.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

1. **The Notification Loop:** Unlocking a smartphone to check a quick piece of information (e.g., local bus arrival times or weather) triggers notification overload, pulling you into social media and breaking focus.
2. **Developer Context Loss:** Long-running AI agent tasks, build scripts, or LLM token usage require constant terminal or browser tab switching, causing context switches and a loss of track of execution costs.
3. **High-Friction Interfaces:** Traditional displays demand active touch or voice interaction when all you need is a passive, ambient glance.

---

## 💡 The Solution

GlanceDesk provides a dedicated, ambient hardware dashboard right on your desk:
- **Zero-Glare E-Paper Display:** High contrast, zero glare under bright lights, and ultra-low power consumption.
- **Always-On Utility:** Live updating without needing taps, swipes, or unlocking a device.
- **Compact & Portable:** Battery-operated so you can move it between your desk, nightstand, or travel setup.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. At-a-Glance Utility Dashboard
- **Public Transport Timings:** Real-time arrival countdowns (e.g., LTA DataMall API).
- **Local Weather & Calendar:** Live temperature, rain alerts, and upcoming agenda items.
- **To-Do Items:** Syncs top daily focus tasks and calendar events. 

### 2. Developer AI Agent Mode
- **Progress Tracking:** Live progress bar for active AI agent runs or terminal processes.
- **Cost & Token Metrics:** Real-time visibility into cumulative API token spend without opening terminal tabs.

### 3. Night & Sleep Mode
- **Auto-Off Night Light:** Warm ambient lighting via an integrated RGB LED ring with custom sleep timers.
- **Sleep Audio:** Plays relaxation tracks or pink noise via an internal micro-speaker.

---

## 👤 Target Audience

- **Developers & Tech Workers:** Needing non-intrusive status tracking for async background tasks.
- **Focus & Productivity Enthusiasts:** Looking to reduce smartphone screen time during deep work blocks.
- **Minimalists:** Wanting functional desk accessories without heavy UI distractions.
